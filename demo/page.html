RainCheck

Will tomorrow's rainfall forecast fail? The model reads the forecast and the recent record of rain that has been verified, and flags days when the day-ahead ECMWF forecast is likely to miss by more than max(5 mm, 50% of the forecast). It answers before the rain falls.

Test results

2,016 city-days from June to November 2025, 12 Indian cities. The model never saw these dates in training. 23.2% of them were failures.
